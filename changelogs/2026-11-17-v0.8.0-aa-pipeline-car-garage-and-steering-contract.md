# Changelog — v0.8.0 (Build 0016)

**Date:** 2026-11-17
**Milestone:** 4-Mode WebGPU Anti-Aliasing Pipeline, Car Garage (AWD/FWD/RWD + Paint), and Multi-Device Steering Contract

## Added
- **4-Mode WebGPU Anti-Aliasing Pipeline (`src/graphics/WebGpuRenderer.hx`, `src/graphics/PostProcessRenderer.hx`, `src/graphics/shaders/`):**
  - **FXAA 3.11 (`src/graphics/RadialBlurShader.hx`)**: Edge-luma luminance smoothing (`sampleFxaa311`) with Low, Medium, and High quality tiers.
  - **SMAA 1x (`SmaaShader.hx`)**: 3-pass morphological pipeline (luma edge detection, bilinear pattern weight estimation, and neighborhood blending) across Low/Med/High tiers while preserving corners and flat UI/terrain planes.
  - **Hardware 4x MSAA (`WebGpuRenderer.hx`)**: Offscreen `sampleCount(4)` color and depth attachments with MSAA geometry pipelines and hardware resolve (`AA Quality` locked to `[DISABLED]` under fixed-4x WebGPU constraints).
  - **TSAA / Temporal Anti-Aliasing (`TaaResolveShader.hx`, `ChaseCamera.hx`, `SoABuffer.hx`)**: 8x/16x/32x Halton sub-pixel jitter projection, per-frame camera/object motion vector reprojection, 3x3 luminance variance/AABB history clamping, and static vs. motion history blending.
  - Added deep visual sawtooth/edge verification suite (`aitests/specs/aa-visual.spec.js`) across Circuit and Sandbox on both Desktop (`1280x720`) and Mobile (`844x390`).

- **Car Selection Garage, Drivetrain Physics Split & Paint Customization (`src/ui/views/carselect/CarSelectView.hx`, `src/physics/CarConfig.hx`, `TireSlipSolver.hx`, `CarMesh.hx`, `WorldManager.hx`, `SaveSystem.hx`):**
  - Added **CarSelect** screen between `MapSelect` and race launch (`UIState.CarSelect`, `#card-car-race_awd`, `#card-car-hatch_fwd`, `#card-car-coupe_rwd`), complete with keyboard/gamepad focus switching (`Left`/`Right`/`Up`/`Down`/`Q`/`E`) and `>=44px` mobile touch swatches.
  - Implemented 3 distinct vehicle archetypes with authentic drivetrain splits, chassis dimensions, steering locks, and procedural 3D proportions:
    - **GT Race Car (`race_awd`)**: AWD (`35%` front / `65%` rear), `2.70m` wheelbase, high aero downforce (`2.50`), planted stability.
    - **Sports Hatch (`hatch_fwd`)**: FWD (`100%` front / `0%` rear), compact `2.42m` wheelbase, sharper `0.66 rad` lock & `0.85` Ackermann factor for tight turning radius (`3.1m`), extended hatch cabin.
    - **Low Sports Coupe (`coupe_rwd`)**: RWD (`0%` front / `100%` rear), low `0.39m` CoG, slammed suspension, `0.62 rad` lock, tail-happy power oversteer rotation.
  - Added 8-swatch paint color picker (`CarConfig.PAINT_PALETTE`) updating showcase/in-game chassis paint and persisting `selectedCarId` / `selectedPaintHex` in `SaveSystem`.

- **Core Anti-Inversion & Tooling Gates (`tests/`, `aitests/specs/controls-direction.spec.js`, `.vscode/`):**
  - Added integrated `InputController -> CarPhysics -> Camera3D` multi-device anti-inversion regression contract covering Keyboard (`WASD` / `Arrows`), Gamepad (`Analog Stick` / `D-Pad`), and Virtual Analog Stick (`TouchControlsHUD` + `#touch-stick-zone` at partial `±45%`, full `±100%`, and continuous Left-to-Right slide).
  - Fixed VSCode `Build & Deploy to IIS` and `Run Unit Tests` tasks (`pwsh.exe` quote-stripping failure) via dedicated `.vscode/build-deploy.ps1` and `.vscode/run-tests.ps1`.

## Fixed
- **Canonical Steering Polarity & Virtual Analog Stick Tracking (`src/input/InputController.hx`, `web/ui-overlay.js`, `tests/input/TestInputController.hx`):**
  - Resolved sign inversion where Left input emitted `+1.0` instead of `-1.0`; enforced canonical `- = Left` (`yaw < 0`, `-X`, Screen Left) and `+ = Right` across digital, analog stick, D-pad, and virtual stick paths.
  - Isolated `#touch-layer` into a dedicated `#touch-root` outside `#ui-root` so live HUD state updates never tear down `#touch-stick-zone` or reset `#touch-stick-knob` mid-drag, wrapped `setPointerCapture` safely, and blocked iOS Safari default touch scrolling.
- **Asset Cache-Busting (`web/web.config`, `web/sw.js`, `web/index.html`):**
  - Added `Cache-Control: no-cache, must-revalidate` for `game.wasm`, `ui-overlay.js`, and `sw.js`, and updated service worker fetch (`no-cache`) + cache version `mob-racing-v0.8.0-r1`.
