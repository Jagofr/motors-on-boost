## Overview

Version **0.2.0** marks a major architectural milestone delivering first-principles arcade raycast vehicle dynamics, an automatic 6-speed powertrain simulation, high-speed radial motion blur post-processing, a procedural atmospheric skybox, dynamic solar lighting at the map summit, refined chase camera physics, and runtime JSON vehicle tuning profiles.

---

## Key Highlights & Systems Implemented

### 1. First-Principles Arcade Raycast Vehicle Dynamics (`CarPhysics.hx`)
- **4-Wheel Independent Spring-Damper Suspension**:
  - Continuous raycast ground surface sampling along track splines.
  - Spring-damper force calculation: `F = (k * (restLength - currentLength) - c * suspVelocity) * contactNormal`.
  - Transverse anti-roll bar torque across opposing wheel pairs (front FL/FR and rear RL/RR) preventing unnatural body roll while preserving realistic suspension travel.
- **Pacejka-Style Tire Friction & Slip Angles**:
  - Separated longitudinal propulsion from lateral cornering grip.
  - Slip angle calculation: `alpha = atan2(vLateral, |vForward|)` clamped through a normalized non-linear grip saturation curve.
- **Arcade Drift State Machine**:
  - Initiation via handbrake or sharp brake-to-steer input at speed.
  - Scaled lateral tire stiffness (35% baseline), counter-steer torque injection, and forward velocity vector biasing to maintain slide momentum without snap-back spinouts.
- **Powertrain Simulation & Automatic Transmission**:
  - Engine flywheel RPM simulation with idle and redline thresholds.
  - Progressive 6-speed forward transmission with automatic up/down-shift logic and shift latency cooldown.
  - Simulated limited-slip differential (LSD) balancing rear driven wheel speeds.

### 2. Runtime Vehicle Configuration Schema (`CarConfig.hx`)
- Typed configuration data structures (`VehicleConfigData`, `SuspensionConfig`, `TireConfig`, `DriftConfig`, `DrivetrainConfig`, `AeroConfig`).
- Full JSON serialization and deserialization (`CarConfig.fromJson`, `CarConfig.toJson`) supporting runtime vehicle tuning without recompilation.

### 3. High-Speed Radial Motion Blur Shader (`RadialBlurShader.hx` & `PostProcessRenderer.hx`)
- Screen-space radial directional blur pass implemented in HxSL (`h3d.shader.ScreenShader`) centered on the vehicle horizon.
- Dynamic speed-dependent intensity scaling smoothly engaging above 140 km/h and during nitrous oxide injection.
- Zero-overhead offscreen render target pass integration into `s3d.renderer`.

### 4. Procedural Atmospheric Skybox (`Skybox.hx`)
- Celestial dome sphere utilizing custom HxSL shader blending deep azure zenith blue (`#1052B8`), bright atmospheric horizon cyan (`#7AC2F2`), and dusk nadir tones.
- Dynamically synchronized with camera translation for an infinite celestial horizon.

### 5. Solar Lighting & Track Scenery Cleanup
- Apex summit solar lighting: repositioned scene directional light to the highest south-east point (`X: +350, Y: -250, Z: 140`).
- Removed scenery skyscraper object at `(X: 295, Y: 130)` that clipped across 9 circuit waypoints without collision.

### 6. Chase Camera Refinement (`ChaseCamera.hx`)
- Spring-damped target lag with dynamic acceleration pull-back during forward surge and nitrous injection.
- Dynamic speed-dependent Field of View (FOV) smoothly expanding from 65° base to 85° at terminal velocity.
- Softened collision trauma impulses: clamped peak trauma ceiling to 0.55 with rapid exponential decay (`3.8 * dt`).

### 7. Persistence & Version Tracking
- Save system schema upgraded to v3 (`mob_motors_on_boost_save_v3`).
- PWA cache name updated to `mob-motors-on-boost-v2-0`.
