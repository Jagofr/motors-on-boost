# Release Notes: v0.5.0 (Build 0011)

**Release Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Target Platform:** WebGL / HTML5, Headless Node.js  
**Compilation Build:** `0011`  

---

## Overview

Milestone **v0.5.0** delivers a complete, clean-room rewrite of the 3D vehicle physics engine and velocity-aligned chase camera in **M.O.B. (Motors on Boost)**. Legacy simcade driving logic was deleted in its entirety and replaced with a modular, pure-simulation **240 Hz 4-wheel raycast vehicle physics architecture** designed around real suspension kinematics, dynamic weight transfer, Pacejka lateral/longitudinal slip curves, combined friction-circle saturation, and critically damped camera tracking.

---

## Key Highlights & Architectural Changes

### 1. Clean-Room Vehicle Physics Configuration (`src/physics/CarConfig.hx`)
- Defined deterministic physical vehicle parameters for chassis (`1450 kg`, `2.70m` wheelbase, `1.96m` track width, `0.48m` CoG height), four-corner suspension (`0.36m` loaded rest length, `0.50m` unloaded / `0.14m` min travel, `25,400 N/m` spring stiffness, asymmetric bump/rebound damping, `14,500 N/m` Anti-Roll Bar), 6-speed gearbox ratios (`[3.42, 2.28, 1.65, 1.28, 1.02, 0.84]`, `3.75` final drive), Pacejka tire constants, and nitrous boost parameters (`7,800 N` thrust force).

### 2. 240 Hz 4-Wheel Raycast Suspension & Anti-Roll Solver (`src/physics/SuspensionSolver.hx`)
- Replaced legacy spring stubs with per-corner raycast height/normal queries against `IGroundProvider` and Oimo static deck colliders:
  - Computes spring compression velocity (`compVel`) via discrete displacement derivative across `240 Hz` fixed physics sub-steps (`FIXED_SUBSTEP = 1.0 / 240.0`).
  - Applies asymmetric bump (`3,950 N·s/m`) and rebound (`5,450 N·s/m`) shock absorber damping.
  - Adds progressive elastomer bump-stop stiffness near minimum strut travel (`minLength = 0.14m`) to prevent hard bottom-outs on big jumps.
  - Solves left-to-right differential axle Anti-Roll Bar (`14,500 N/m`) load coupling on both front and rear axles and resolves dynamic chassis pitch and roll directly from suspension compression differentials.

### 3. 6-Speed Automatic Drivetrain & Boost Solver (`src/physics/DrivetrainSolver.hx`)
- Implemented multi-gear transmission (`Gear -1` Reverse, `Gears 1–6` Forward) with hysteresis upshift/downshift RPM thresholds (`6,900` / `2,850 RPM`) and reverse engagement interlock requiring near-standstill speeds (`<= 1.2 m/s`).
- Added smooth sinusoidal-bell engine torque curve mapping against RPM (`peakTorqueNm = 560 N·m`), final drive gear multiplication, viscous wheelspin delta tracking (`rearWheelSpinDelta`), front-biased hydraulic braking (`62% / 38%`), and rear-locking handbrake support.
- Separated `nitroThrust` cleanly from wheel torque so nitrous boost accelerates smoothly along the terrain tangent vector without saturating 1st-gear longitudinal tire friction limits.

### 4. Pacejka Tire Slip, Friction Circle & Slope Stiction Solver (`src/physics/TireSlipSolver.hx`)
- Computes per-wheel vertical normal load ($F_z$) with dynamic longitudinal pitch weight transfer ($\pm 0.5 \cdot (h_{\text{cg}} / L) \cdot m \cdot a_x$) and sporty `35% / 65%` AWD tractive torque distribution.
- Models speed-blended **Ackermann steering geometry** (`ackermannFactor = 0.65`, inner front wheel steers tighter at low speed; smoothly converges with `highSpeedSteerDamp = 0.026` at highway speeds).
- Evaluates first-order relaxation-filtered slip angles (`relaxationLength = 0.34m`), **Pacejka Magic Formula** lateral force curves ($B = 9.2, C = 1.52, D = 1.08, E = -0.35$), **combined Friction Circle** clamping ($\sqrt{F_x^2 + F_y^2} \le \mu F_z$), and low-speed 2D static slope stiction (`isStictionActive`) so parked vehicles hold steady on steep hills without creeping downward.

### 5. Rigid-Body Coordinator & Velocity-Aligned Chase Camera (`src/physics/CarPhysics.hx`, `src/camera/ChaseCamera.hx`)
- Unified Oimo `RigidBody` velocity synchronization (`syncToOimoBody` / `syncFromOimoBody`) around the `240 Hz` solver loop, maintaining airborne ballistic trajectories and zero per-frame heap allocations.
- Implemented a spring-damper `SmoothDamp` velocity-aligned `ChaseCamera` that tracks vehicle velocity at speed, transitions cleanly when reversing or parked, adds subtle lateral-G cornering banking (`MAX_BANK_RAD = 0.055`), expands FOV smoothly with speed and nitrous boost, and raycasts terrain elevation to maintain minimum camera ground clearance (`MIN_GROUND_CLEARANCE = 0.95m`).

### 6. Skill Specification & Physics Unit Verification (`tests/physics/`, `tests/camera/`)
- Completely rewrote `.agents/skills/vehicle-physics-and-camera/SKILL.md` to match the new 4-wheel raycast coordinate and solver pipeline.
- Added comprehensive unit test suites in `tests/physics/TestCarConfig.hx`, `tests/physics/TestCarPhysics.hx` (equilibrium, slope stiction, multi-gear acceleration, reverse interlock, left/right steering symmetry, smooth ramp climb without catapulting, surface friction grip, boost recharge), and `tests/camera/TestChaseCamera.hx`.
