# Release Notes: v0.5.1 (Build 0012)

**Release Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Target Platform:** WebGL / HTML5, Desktop & Mobile Browsers  
**Compilation Build:** `0012`  

---

## Overview

Milestone **v0.5.1** refines real-world driving feel on top of the **v0.5.0** 4-wheel raycast simulation by calibrating digital, analog gamepad, and virtual touch steering polarity and sensitivity, enriching road and off-road surface materials across both playable maps, and eliminating Circuit guardrail ground-clipping when riding along barriers.

---

## Key Changes

### 1. Steering Polarity Calibration & Sensitivity Boost (`src/input/InputController.hx`)
- **Unified Left/Right Polarity Fix**:
  - Flipped digital keyboard (`A` / `Left Arrow`), gamepad D-pad/left thumbstick (`stickX`), and virtual touch steering wheel (`touchSteer`) output signs to match in-engine right-handed chassis yaw orientation during live gameplay across all three input backends.
- **Base Sensitivity Scaling (`1.20x`)**:
  - Scaled final conditioned `steer` output against `1.20 * steeringSensitivity` (clamped cleanly within `[-1.0, 1.0]`), preserving responsive turn-in while retaining fine user-configurable sensitivity slider control (`0%`–`100%`).
- Updated `tests/input/TestInputController.hx` and `aitests/run-playwright-tests.js` Scenario 5 assertions to match the calibrated polarity across `keyboard`, `gamepad`, and `touch`.

### 2. Multi-Surface Road & Off-Road Material Friction Zones (`src/world/Track.hx`, `src/world/SandboxMap.hx`)
- **Highway Circuit Material Bands (`src/world/Track.hx`)**:
  - Split `Track.getGroundFriction(x, y)` into distinct grip bands that directly feed `TireSlipSolver` per wheel:
    - **Clean Racing Asphalt** (`|d| <= 8.8m`): full `config.asphaltFriction` (`1.00`).
    - **Painted Outer Kerb / Rumble Strip** (`8.8m < |d| <= 10.0m`): `0.88 * asphaltFriction` for authentic threshold limit feedback on corner exits.
    - **Gravel / Dirt Shoulder** (`|d| > 10.0m`): reduced `0.55` traction.
- **Sandbox Proving Grounds Surface Materials & Mesh Detail (`src/world/SandboxMap.hx`)**:
  - Upgraded `SandboxMap.getGroundFriction(x, y)` with localized physics materials matching visual zones:
    - Central flat **Dry Asphalt Skidpad** (`r <= 36m`): `1.00`.
    - **Wet Polished Transition Apron** (`36m < r <= 52m`): slick `0.44` low-grip ring for controlled drifting and friction-circle testing.
    - **Concrete Stunt Launch Ramp** & **High-Speed Banked Bowl**: high-grip `1.05` / `1.00`.
    - **Loose Dirt Moguls**: `0.58` off-road slip surface.
    - **Packed Dirt Mountain Climb**: `0.62` steep-grade dirt traction.
  - Increased sandbox terrain heightfield tessellation from `64` to `96` divisions for crisper visual slope alignment with analytical raycasts.

### 3. Circuit Guardrail Ground-Clipping Fix (`src/world/Track.hx`)
- **Root Cause Fixed**:
  - Previously, `Track.enforceBoundaries` clamped the vehicle center to `halfWidth - 0.3` (`9.7m`), while outer tire hubs (`±0.98m` X, `±1.35m` Y) reached `10.68m..11.3m` — falling straight off the `HALF_WIDTH = 10.0m` cliff (`-2.5m` exterior drop) during suspension raycasts and pulling the chassis below road height.
- **Roadbed Support Shelf & Floor Clamp**:
  - Extended the flat roadbed elevation shelf in `Track.getGroundElevation` out to `HALF_WIDTH + 1.8m` (`11.8m`) before sloping to `-2.5m`, ensuring all 4 raycast suspension struts stay supported on solid flat surface while riding flush against barriers.
  - Updated `Track.getGroundNormal` to sample longitudinal elevation along centerline projection points (`proj.centerPoint ± segDir * eps`) so lateral wheel proximity never tilts surface normals sideways into the embankment.
  - Updated `Track.enforceBoundaries` to clamp vehicle lateral offset at `config.halfWidth - 0.75` (`9.25m`) and clamp `pos.z >= proj.z + ROAD_LIFT` on barrier scrape contact.
- Added automated Circuit guardrail elevation, normal, and Playwright E2E anti-clipping verification in `tests/world/TestTrack.hx` and `aitests/run-playwright-tests.js`.
