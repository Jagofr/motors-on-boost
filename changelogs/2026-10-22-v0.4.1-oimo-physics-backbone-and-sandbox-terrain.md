# Release Notes: v0.4.1 (Build 0007)

**Release Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Target Target:** WebGL / HTML5, Headless Node.js (Edge / Chrome / Mobile Browsers)  
**Compilation Build:** `0007`  

---

## Overview

Milestone **v0.4.1** marks a major architectural upgrade to M.O.B. (Motors on Boost) by integrating **OimoPhysics** as the 3D rigid-body collision and dynamics backbone, driving it directly with our established first-principles multi-link raycast suspension, Pacejka slip-angle tire adhesion, and Criterion-style (*Need for Speed: Most Wanted 2012*) arcade drift mechanics.

This release also resolves missing ground textures on the Open Sandbox Proving Grounds map, corrects polygon triangle winding orders, adds procedural cyber grid coordinates, eliminates boundary Z-locking, conforms chassis pitch/roll to local vehicle axes, and corrects the version numbering scheme.

---

## Key Highlights & Architectural Changes

### 1. OimoPhysics 3D Rigid Body Collision Backbone (`src/physics/CarPhysics.hx`)
- **Native Haxe Physics Engine Integration**:
  - Integrated `saharan/OimoPhysics` (Haxe 4.3+ native port) with zero external JavaScript extern friction or GC allocation churn.
  - Initialized `physicsWorld` with gravity $(0, 0, -9.81\text{ m/s}^2)$ matching Heaps.io world coordinate axes.
  - Instantiated an inertial 3D rigid body (`chassisBody`, mass: $1350\text{ kg}$) with a `BoxGeometry` collider ($1.84\text{m} \times 2.8\text{m} \times 0.44\text{m}$) matching the visual `CarMesh` hierarchy.
- **Actuated by Established Mathematical Formulation**:
  - 4-wheel independent raycast suspension computes compression $F_{\text{susp}} = k(L_0 - L) - c v_{\text{susp}}$ and applies real upward 3D forces at the wheel world contact points.
  - Drive propulsion and braking forces are applied along the vehicle's forward vector.
  - Lateral tire cornering grip is evaluated via Pacejka slip-angle saturation curves:
    $$\mu_{\text{lat}} = \frac{2 \alpha / \alpha_{\text{peak}}}{1 + (\alpha / \alpha_{\text{peak}})^2}$$
  - Active drift state machine ("Brake-to-Drift") scales lateral stiffness down to 35%, applies counter-steer torque assist, and biases forward velocity momentum to prevent spinouts while sliding at 200+ km/h.
  - Aerodynamic downforce and quadratic drag push onto the rigid body.
- **Crash & Impact Mechanics**:
  - Wall and obstacle collisions now benefit from Oimo's projected Gauss-Seidel constraint solver, providing authentic momentum transfer, physical restitution, and barrier impacts.

### 2. Sandbox Proving Grounds Ground Texture & Winding Fix (`src/world/SandboxMap.hx`)
- **Procedural Cyber Grid Texture**:
  - Generated a procedural 256x256 ARGB coordinate grid texture with deep slate-charcoal asphalt background (`#222C28`), crisp coordinate lines (`#3E5048`), and high-visibility electric cyan intersection nodes (`#00E5FF`).
  - Applied texture with `wrap = Repeat` across the entire 400m x 400m terrain mesh.
- **Winding Order & Backface Culling**:
  - Fixed triangle winding from clockwise (`i0, i2, i1`) to counter-clockwise (`i0, i1, i2` and `i1, i3, i2`), ensuring surface normals point upward ($+Z$) and eliminating WebGL backface-culling invisibility.
  - Set `terrainMesh.material.mainPass.culling = None` for complete rendering reliability.
  - Added UV texture coordinates to `Polygon.uvs` ($16\text{m}$ coordinate interval tiling).
- **Physical Boundary Colliders**:
  - Registered 4 static Oimo boundary wall rigid bodies at $X, Y = \pm 195\text{m}$.
  - Removed erroneous `pos.z = getGroundElevation(...)` override from boundary clamping, enabling airborne ballistic flight and jumping off ramps.

### 3. Local Chassis 3D Pitch/Roll Alignment (`src/Main.hx`)
- Fixed orientation update order so pitch and roll rotate locally around the vehicle heading rather than around fixed world axes:
  ```haxe
  playerMesh.setRotation(0, 0, carPhysics.yaw + carPhysics.driftAngle);
  playerMesh.rotate(carPhysics.pitch, 0, 0); // Local pitch
  playerMesh.rotate(0, carPhysics.roll, 0);  // Local roll
  ```
  This eliminates sideways tilting when driving on hills or curved ramps facing arbitrary headings.

### 4. Version Number Correction (`src/Version.hx`)
- Corrected semantic version from accidental major bump (`1.0.0`) back down to `0`, bumped minor version to `4`, and bumped patch to `1`: **`v0.4.1 (Build 0007)`**.
- Tallied compilation build counter from 5 to 7 (`0007`).
- Updated PWA service worker cache name to `mob-motors-on-boost-v0-4-1`.
