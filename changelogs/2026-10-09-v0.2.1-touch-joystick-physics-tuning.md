# M.O.B. (Motors on Boost) — Release Notes v0.2.1

- **Release Date**: October 9, 2026
- **Version**: `0.2.1`
- **Build**: `Build 4`
- **Channel**: Alpha
- **Target Platform**: HTML5 / WebGL 2.0 (Heaps.io 2.1.0, Haxe 4.3.7)

---

## Overview

Version **0.2.1** introduces an ergonomic overhaul of mobile virtual touch driving controls, true multi-touch concurrency supporting simultaneous chords (Gas + Steer, Gas + Nitro, Gas + Drift, Drift + Nitro, Brake + Nitro), runtime-tunable driving physics parameters (`tireSlipBias`, `driftTriggerAngle`, `bodyRollScale`, `groundFriction`, `wheelspinAmount`), a dedicated handbrake breakaway mechanism, and visual rear-tire burnout animations.

## Key Highlights & Systems Overhaul

### 1. Ergonomic Multi-Touch Driving Controls (`TouchControlsHUD.hx`)
- **Virtual Analog Steering Joystick (Left Thumb)**:
  - Replaced discrete `<` and `>` buttons with a proportional 2D analog stick located in the lower-left corner (`R_base = 65px`, `R_knob = 30px`, `maxTravel = 50px`).
  - Proportional horizontal steering deflection: `touchSteer = clamp(dx / maxTravel, -1.0, 1.0)`.
  - Continuous knob tracking with automatic spring-back return to neutral on touch release.
  - Directional tick indicators and glowing cyan HUD rendering.
- **Nitrous Boost Button (Left Index / Fingers)**:
  - Placed directly above the steering joystick (`Y: sh - 230px`), allowing the left hand to steer with the thumb while tapping or holding Nitro with index or middle fingers.
- **High-Capacity Gas Thumbpad (Right Thumb)**:
  - Enlarged 88px diameter circular pad positioned in the prime right-thumb rest zone (`sw - 120px, sh - 110px`) with generous hit hysteresis to prevent accidental release.
- **Arched Drift & Service Brake Buttons (Right Fingers)**:
  - **Drift / Handbrake Pad**: Positioned directly above Gas and Brake (`sw - 165px, sh - 225px`) for effortless drift triggering while keeping the right thumb planted on Gas.
  - **Brake Pad**: Positioned adjacent to Gas (`sw - 230px, sh - 110px`) for rapid braking or reverse gear engagement.
- **True Multi-Touch Concurrency & Event Robustness**:
  - Eliminated Heaps `Interactive` hierarchy from touch capsules to prevent scene graph focus capture and multi-touch dropouts.
  - Independent per-touch pointer identifier mapping (`activeRoles: Map<Int, String>`) ensuring fingers never cancel or deselect each other.
  - Native DOM TouchEvent integration on WebGL canvas (`touchstart`, `touchmove`, `touchend`, `touchcancel`) with `passive: false` and `preventDefault()` to suppress browser gesture interruptions.
  - Verified simultaneous 4-finger chords: Steer Stick + Gas + Nitro + Drift.

### 2. Runtime Tunable Driving Physics Architecture (`CarPhysics.hx` & `CarConfig.hx`)
- **Tire Slip Bias (`tireSlipBias: Float`)**:
  - Normalized range `[-1.0 .. 1.0]`:
    - `[-1.0 .. 0.0)`: Rear bias (oversteer) — rear tires lose lateral adhesion first, facilitating agile drift initiation and responsive slides.
    - `0.0`: Neutral bias — balanced slip angles across front and rear axles.
    - `(0.0 .. 1.0]`: Front bias (understeer) — front tires break traction first, stabilizing the tail and pushing wide in turns.
  - Dynamically modulates axle grip multipliers: `frontGripMult = (1.0 - bias * 0.40) * groundFriction`, `rearGripMult = (1.0 + bias * 0.40) * groundFriction`.
- **Proper Handbrake Function & Traction Breakaway**:
  - Handbrake actuation immediately cuts rear lateral grip (`rearGripMult *= 0.20 + (bias + 1.0) * 0.08`), applies longitudinal drag, and injects yaw impulse to swing the chassis into the turn.
- **Drift Trigger Angle Threshold (`driftTriggerAngle: Float`)**:
  - Configurable slip angle threshold (default `0.22 rad` / `~12.6°`). When vehicle angle relative to velocity reaches or exceeds this angle, full arcade drift physics engage automatically.
- **Dynamic Body Roll Scaling (`bodyRollScale: Float`)**:
  - Tunable roll scalar (default `1.0`) directly affecting 4-wheel independent suspension spring compression and extension.
  - Anti-roll bar torque and centrifugal roll ceiling clamp (`maxRoll = 0.25 rad`) prevent flat-road roll-overs while allowing road bumps and curb raycasts to pitch and roll the chassis dynamically.
- **Ground Friction Scaling (`groundFriction: Float`)**:
  - Global coefficient scaling tire longitudinal traction, lateral cornering stiffness, rolling resistance, and braking deceleration.
- **Tire Wheelspin Simulation (`wheelspinAmount: Float`)**:
  - Standstill full-throttle burnout wheelspin (`speed < 8 m/s && throttle > 0.75`) flaring rear wheel spin velocity and engine RPM.
  - Harsh cornering power oversteer wheelspin (`steer > 0.25 && throttle > 0.75`) breaking rear traction and tightening yaw into turns.
- **Public Tunable Accessors**:
  - Directly exposed on `CarPhysics`: `tireSlipBias`, `driftTriggerAngle`, `bodyRollScale`, `groundFriction`, `wheelspinAmount`, `baseAcceleration`, `boostAcceleration`.

### 3. Visual Polish & Animation (`Main.hx`)
- **Burnout Wheelspin Animation**:
  - Decoupled rear wheel mesh rotation (`rearWheelSpinAngle`) from vehicle ground speed, incorporating dynamic `wheelSpinVelocity` to visually spin rear tires during burnout launches and drift slides.
