## Executive Summary
Milestone release **v0.3.0 (Build 0005)** advances M.O.B. (Motors on Boost) with a complete overhaul of the chase camera speed dynamics, integration of native touch icon assets, dual-tier headless and in-browser automated verification, graphics options dependency enforcement, and decoupled high-resolution UI rendering:

1. **Dynamic Speed Chase Camera (`ChaseCamera.hx`)**:
   - Replaced excessive physical camera trailing at high velocities with a tightly bounded, subtle lag (+0.85m at 270+ km/h).
   - Implemented forward velocity feedforward (`0.65`) and speed-scaled spring stiffness (`15.0 + speedRatio * 4.0`) to keep the camera anchored closely behind the vehicle chassis.
   - Replaced linear, extreme FOV widening (which previously reached 90° and caused severe fisheye warping) with a smooth non-linear expansion curve (`pow(speedRatio, 1.25)`) bounded between `65.0°` (base) and `77.0°` (top speed cruising) with `+3.5°` nitrous extra (peak ~80.5°).
   - Tightened baseline vehicle framing (`BASE_DISTANCE = 7.5m`, `BASE_HEIGHT = 2.8m`, `LOOK_AHEAD_DIST = 6.2m`).
   - Maintained zero runtime heap allocations in update loops.

2. **Touch Controls Icon Assets Integration & HUD Alignment (`TouchControlsHUD.hx`, `res/icons/`)**:
   - Integrated loaded button icons from `res/icons/` (`burn.png`, `gas-pedal.png`, `brake.png`, `hand-brake.png`) with automatic fallback to high-contrast procedural vector graphics.
   - Structured ergonomic two-handed virtual controls: left-hand virtual analog steering joystick with upper-left Nitro button; right-hand enlarged Gas pad with upper-right Brake and arched Drift controls.
   - Full multi-touch concurrency across all simultaneous chords (Steer + Gas + Nitro + Drift).

3. **Dual-Tier Automated Testing Infrastructure (`tests/` & `web/test-suite.html`)**:
   - **Headless Unit Test Suite (`tests/`)**: Built on `utest` 1.13.2 with VSCode `Haxe Test Explorer` compatibility, featuring 200/200 assertions passing across 12 test fixtures in under 0.06s.
   - **Modular Test Runner Filtering (`TestMain.hx`)**: Added module and class argument filtering (`node tests/node-runner.js camera`, `physics`, etc.).
   - **Automated Browser Visual/Programmatic Testing Suite (`tests/run-browser-tests.js` & `web/test-suite.html`)**: Driven headlessly via Microsoft Edge and Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP), testing 6 end-to-end scenarios (15/15 passed).

4. **Graphics Options Hierarchy & Dependency Graying Out (`GraphicsOptionsPanel.hx`)**:
   - Dependent settings (Shadow Buffer Size, Anisotropy Sampling, Anti-Aliasing Multiplier, and Reflection Updates) automatically disable, label `[DISABLED]`, and reject user modifications when parent options are disabled.
   - Added vertical viewport scrolling (`scrollOffset`, `maxVisibleRows`) and adaptive card backplates.

5. **Granular Steering Sensitivity Across All Tabs (`ControlsOptionsPanel.hx`)**:
   - Permanent steering sensitivity slider at the top of Keyboard, Gamepad, and Touch tabs (0% to 100% in 2% steps), synchronized directly to `InputController.steerSensitivity`.

6. **Dedicated Touch Controls Options Tab (`ControlsOptionsPanel.hx`, `SaveSystem.hx`)**:
   - Added dedicated 5th Options tab supporting Default vector layout schematic and Custom offset/scale adjustment with 1-click Reset to Defaults.

7. **Decoupled 3D Render Scale from 2D UI Resolution (`PostProcessRenderer.hx`, `GraphicsManager.hx`)**:
   - 3D scene renders to dedicated offscreen render target scaled by `renderScale` (0.25x to 4.0x) with dynamically allocated matching `Depth24Stencil8` depth targets, keeping 2D UI canvas and text at native 1:1 window resolution.

## Detailed Systems Breakdown

### 1. Speed Camera Dynamics & FOV Curve
```
   Stationary (0 km/h)  ──>  BASE_FOV: 65.0°, Distance: 7.5m
   Cruising (180 km/h)  ──>  FOV: ~71.5°,     Distance: ~7.9m
   Top Speed (270 km/h) ──>  FOV: 77.0°,      Distance: 8.35m (+0.85m lag)
   Full Nitrous Boost   ──>  FOV: 80.5°,      Distance: 8.70m (+0.35m pull-back)
```
- **Integrator Lag Mitigation**: Without velocity feedforward, spring-damper camera followers lag proportional to velocity ($x_{\text{lag}} \approx v / k$). At 75 m/s, this caused the camera to trail 8+ meters behind the car. In v0.3.0, `fwdVel * clampedDt * 0.65` feeds vehicle momentum directly into `currentPos`, holding camera distance to within `+0.85m` of the target resting distance.
- **Controlled Longitudinal Punch**: Acceleration pull-back responds smoothly to sudden velocity differentials (`currentAccel > 1.5 m/s²`), clamped tightly to `0.55m` (+0.35m under nitrous injection).
