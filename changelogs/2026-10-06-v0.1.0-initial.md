---

## 1. Core Engine & Platform Architecture
- **Language & Pipeline:** Built using Haxe 4.3+ targeting JavaScript via Heaps.io (`h3d` for 3D physics, track, and camera; `h2d` for HUD and UI overlays).
- **Compilation Target:** Output compiled to `web/game.js` via `compile.hxml` using `-D js-es=6` optimizations.
- **PWA Integration:** Full Progressive Web App manifest (`web/manifest.json`) and Service Worker (`web/sw.js`) supporting installable, fullscreen, landscape-locked gameplay.
- **Display & Sizing:** Integrated high-DPI canvas synchronization via `s2d.checkResize()` and `gfx.applyRenderScale()`, eliminating recursive window resize call stack overflow exceptions.
- **Fallback Shell:** Styled neon `<noscript>` alert card informing players when JavaScript or WebGL is disabled.

---

## 2. Vehicle Physics & Camera Systems
- **Chassis & Suspension:** Implemented custom raycast-style suspension with compression springs, roll/pitch settling, and dynamic ground clamping (`Track.ROAD_LIFT`).
- **Friction & Drift Dynamics:** Multi-component tire friction combining longitudinal acceleration, engine braking, rolling drag, and lateral slip velocity. Added handbrake-initiated power slides and drift angle tracking.
- **Procedural Vehicle Chassis:** Multi-part 3D mesh chassis (`CarMesh.hx`) with steerable front wheels, emissive headlights, and dynamic body materials.
- **Spring-Arm Chase Camera (`ChaseCamera.hx`):** Smooth third-person chase camera featuring velocity-aligned look-ahead, speed-dependent FOV stretching, and non-linear trauma shake decay on drift initiation and nitro boost.

---

## 3. World Generation & Highway Traffic
- **Endless Spline Track (`Track.hx`):** Procedural multi-lane asphalt highway generation with lane markers, guard rails, and infinite waypoint tracking.
- **Traffic System (`TrafficSystem.hx`):** Highway traffic state machine managing multi-lane vehicle flow, speed variation, overtaking routines, and player collision boundaries.

---

## 4. Audio Pipeline & Synthesizer
- **Web Audio Engine (`AudioManager.hx`):** Dependency-free procedural audio engine synthesizing engine combustion frequencies, tire screech white noise filters, and low-frequency nitro resonance.
- **Volume Staging:** Independent gain nodes for Master, Music, and Sound Effects (SFX) with persistent volume levels.

---

## 5. UI, Typography & Procedural Iconography
- **Procedural Vector Icons (`UIIcons.hx`):** Vector icon rasterizer using `h2d.Graphics` rendered directly into cached `h2d.Tile` textures. Bypasses variable TTF glyph extraction issues and enables dynamic tinting via `bitmap.color.setColor()`.
- **Options & Controls Menu:** Multi-tabbed menu (`Graphics`, `Audio`, `Keyboard`, `Gamepad`) supporting edge-triggered bumper switching (`LB`/`RB`, `Q`/`E`), discrete volume step bars (`■`/`□`), and device isolation toggles (`Both`, `KeyboardOnly`, `GamepadOnly`).
- **Typography:** Configured `StackSansHeadline` for headings/branding alongside standard monospace HUD readouts.
- **Credits Screen:** Complete attribution screen honoring the lead developer, in-house publishing by RvL // ROGUE, and AI architecture assistance by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash.

---

## 6. Sequential Bootloader & Preloader
- **Delta-Time State Machine:** Sequential boot flow (`Studio` $\to$ `Engine` $\to$ `Disclaimer` $\to$ `Showcase Fade-in` $\to$ `Splash / InGameHUD`).
- **Interaction Rules:**
  - Screen 1 (Studio Splash) & Screen 2 (Engine Splash): Skippable via keypress or click with accelerated fade-out.
  - Screen 3 (Disclaimer): Strictly locked for a minimum of 5.5 seconds (`disclaimerMinDuration`) while legal notices, IP disclosures, and ecosystem logos are displayed.
- **Background Asset Pre-loading:** 3D scene elements, audio singletons, and physics systems allocate asynchronously while Screen 3 holds an opaque black backdrop.
- **Canvas WebP Decoding:** Custom offscreen HTML5 Canvas pipeline (`loadWebpTile`) to decode 1080p `.webp` splash assets into raw RGBA buffers and bind them to `h3d.mat.Texture`.
- **Showcase Cross-Fade:** Smooth 1.2-second transition using an overlay curtain (`showcaseCurtain`) fading out to reveal the rotating vehicle showcase and pulsating splash prompt.
