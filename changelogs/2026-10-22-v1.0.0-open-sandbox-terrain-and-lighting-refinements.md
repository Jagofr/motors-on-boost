## Executive Summary
Milestone **v1.0.0 (Build 0006)** represents a major architectural milestone for M.O.B. (Motors on Boost), introducing:
1. **Open Sandbox Proving Grounds**: A 400m x 400m continuous 3D terrain environment featuring rolling sinusoidal moguls, steep 12.5m apex hill climb, high-speed launch ramp with ballistic airborne drop-off, a 25-degree banked high-speed bowl, central flat staging skidpad, and invisible perimeter collision walls.
2. **Highway Street Lighting & Soft Shadow Pipeline**: Full procedural high-mast highway luminaires lining the circuit perimeter, coupled with boosted ambient lighting profiles and secondary directional sky fill to completely eliminate harsh pitch-black shadow contrasts.
3. **Unified Traffic Collision Dynamics**: Harmonized front traffic collisions (head-on / oncoming) to use the identical responsive impulse and momentum transfer logic as rear-end traffic collisions (0.75x player forward speed retention, speed boost transfer to civilian vehicles, and 0.35 camera trauma).
4. **Universal Ground Architecture (`IGroundProvider`)**: Decoupled 3D world driving surfaces from spline-only constraints, enabling analytical continuous elevation raycasting, finite-difference normal gradient evaluation, and dynamic 4-wheel chassis slope conforming (terrain pitch & roll).
5. **Major Version Bump**: Incremented semantic versioning to `1.0.0`, cleared patch counter, and tallied compilation build counter to `0006`.

## Key Features & Improvements

### 1. Open Sandbox Proving Grounds (`src/world/SandboxMap.hx`)
- **Procedural Heightfield Terrain**: 400m x 400m analytical terrain surface evaluated at $O(1)$ zero-allocation speed per wheel contact.
- **Center Skidpad**: Flat, high-traction circular tarmac ($R \le 35\text{m}$) for zero-to-sixty testing, donuts, and drift angle calibration.
- **Rolling Moguls Section**: Sinusoidal washboards ($X \in [35, 140], Y \in [-50, 50]$) oscillating up to $\pm 1.35\text{m}$ to exercise 4-wheel spring-damper frequencies and anti-roll bar torsional resistance.
- **Apex Hill Climb**: Gaussian elevation peak rising to $+12.5\text{m}$ at $(-95, 95)$ to stress-test uphill drivetrain torque, automatic downshifting under load, and downhill braking dive.
- **Airborne Launch Ramp**: Inclined wedge rising from $0\text{m}$ to $+3.8\text{m}$ over 50 meters, culminating in a sharp crest lip that launches vehicles into freefall ballistic airborne trajectory with suspension droop and heavy landing compression.
- **Banked High-Speed Bowl**: Parabolic curved berm banked at $25^\circ$ rising to $+5.2\text{m}$ elevation for lateral-G grip testing and high-speed drift breakaway.
- **Invisible Perimeter Walls**: Elastic boundary constraints at $X, Y = \pm 195\text{m}$ that clamp vehicle position and apply momentum-conserving bumper restitution.
- **Perimeter Floodlight Towers**: 4 quad-fixture high-mast stadium floodlights illuminating the proving grounds from all quadrants.

### 2. Highway Street Lights & Shadow Contrast Refinements (`src/world/Track.hx`, `src/graphics/GraphicsManager.hx`)
- **Procedural Highway Luminaires**: 16+ steel luminaire poles lining the outer Armco guardrails with cantilevered mast arms, protective luminaire casings, and warm golden-white phosphor lenses (`0xFFF2B0`).
- **Soft Ambient Fill**: Upgraded ambient light across Low, Medium, High, and Ultra settings in `GraphicsManager.hx` (`0x505F70` through `0x94ABC2`), preventing pitch-black crevice shadows.
- **Secondary Sky-Bounce Fill Light**: Balanced directional fill light (`0x55687C`) opposite primary sunlight, giving vehicle chassis and terrain slopes soft, realistic diffuse lighting.

### 3. Unified Front & Rear Traffic Collision Dynamics (`src/entities/TrafficSystem.hx`)
- **Momentum-Conserving Impulse**: Both head-on/front and rear traffic collisions now preserve player forward speed at `0.75x` (eliminating punitive 0.4x dead stops), impart dynamic velocity to civilian cars, and inject controlled `0.35` camera trauma.

### 4. 4-Wheel Terrain Conforming (`src/physics/CarPhysics.hx`)
- **Dynamic Terrain Pitch**: Calculated from front vs rear axle elevation differential ($\Delta z / 2.7\text{m}$).
- **Dynamic Terrain Roll**: Calculated from left vs right wheel elevation differential ($\Delta z / 1.96\text{m}$).
- Seamlessly tilts and rolls chassis when driving on hill slopes, ramps, and banked berms.
